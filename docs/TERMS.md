## Unofficial app

GradeGupta is an independent app. It is **not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by PowerSchool, Home Access Center, or any school district**. Home Access Center is a trademark of its owner. Grades and calculations shown in the app are unofficial estimates. Your school's records are the official ones.

## Use your own account

- **Whose account:** only sign in with a Home Access Center account you are allowed to use. That means your own, or your child's as their parent or guardian.
- **District rules:** follow your school district's acceptable-use rules for HAC.
- **Other people's records:** don't use the app to access anyone else's records without permission.
- **Load on HAC:** GradeGupta checks HAC no more often than your phone allows background apps to run, plus when you refresh. Don't use it in a way that overloads your district's website.

## Estimates, not official grades

The following are calculated by the app and may be wrong, for example if your district uses a different formula or HAC changes:

- averages and GPA
- what-if results
- final exam calculations
- "hidden grade" estimates

**Always confirm important grades with your teacher or school.** Don't make decisions, such as skipping an exam, based only on this app.
