# GradeGupta Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 21, 2026_

> This policy describes how the app actually works. It was not written by a lawyer; have it reviewed before publishing to an app store.

## The short version

GradeGupta has **no servers, no accounts with us, no ads, no analytics and no tracking**. Your Home Access Center (HAC) login and your grades stay on your phone. The developers never receive them.

## What is stored on your phone

- Your district's HAC address, plus your HAC username and password. These are kept in your phone's secure keychain (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore).
- Copies of your grades, grade history, planner to-dos and app settings, in a local database on your phone. On Android this database is excluded from cloud backups.

## Where your data goes

- **Only to your school district's HAC website**, over encrypted HTTPS, to sign in and load your pages. This is the same as using HAC in a browser. Nothing is sent to the developers or to any third party.
- Grade alerts are created on your phone. They are not sent through a push notification server.

## Deleting your data

- Removing an account in Settings deletes its saved password and all of its saved grades from your phone.
- Uninstalling the app deletes everything.

## Students and children

- GradeGupta is meant for students and their parents or guardians.
- No personal information is sent to us, so we do not collect information from children (COPPA).
- Students under 13 should use the app with a parent's or guardian's permission.
- Education records travel directly from the district to the user's own device. We never receive, store or share them (FERPA).
